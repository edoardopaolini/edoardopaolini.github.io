Biography

{%- assign portrait = site.static_files | where: 'path', '/assets/img/portrait.jpg' | first -%}

I received a B.Sc. in Computer Science from the University of Verona, Italy (2020), and an M.Sc. in Quantitative and Computational Biology from the University of Trento, Italy (2023). I am now a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Verona.

My doctoral work aims to develop EEG and TMS-EEG systems based on brain connectomics for the personalized treatment of epilepsy.

My interests span computer science, biology and neuroscience: computational models, brain connectivity and signal source estimation.
